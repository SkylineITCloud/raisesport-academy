About RaiseSport Academy

Building a sustainable ecosystem for Football,sport & entertainment

We are a Non-Profit Company and Public Benefit Organisation creating real socio-economic value and It aims to identify, develop, and showcase sporting talent (with a current focus on football) for Athletes and teams. .

Our Vision

Every talented junior athlete in Umlazi and Durban is seen and sponsored .

Our Mission

Find talent from Townships , Rural areas and in schools; develop and connect them to sponsors .

Our Values

Discipline and consistency; creativity and enterprise in funding athletes .

Enterprise Funding Innovative Creativity Discipline Consistency
What We Do

Services built for athletes, teams & brands

A wide range of professional services to support every corner of the sports industry - from grassroots talent to national stage.

Tournament Fees & Leagues

Comprehensive league structures and tournaments with transparent, affordable fees - from 5-a-side futsal to full-format competition.

Coaching Programs

Professional coaching that develops athletes' skills, physical conditioning and mental toughness for peak performance.

Sport Marketing

Management and representation for athletes and teams - including contract negotiations, branding and career planning.

Fundraising Platform

Our innovative health and fundraising platform connects athletes with supporters and sponsors for real financial backing.

Sport Merchandise

High-quality branded merchandise that helps teams and athletes build their brand and generate additional revenue.

Event Hosting

Professional organisation and hosting of sporting events - from local tournaments to national competitions.

Brand Partnerships

Strategic connections between athletes, teams and brands for mutually beneficial sponsorship opportunities.

Sport Consulting

Expert advice and strategic planning for sports organisations, teams and individual athletes.

Administration & Governance

Support for clubs and administrators - compliance, structures and operations that make sport organisations sustainable.
